# Phase 3C data limitations

The six KPI cards now use the selected Dixie step. Time retains unknown windows;
area retains approximate workbook units. The 5:12 PM dimensions (40 × 40 ft)
are displayed as approximately 1,600 sq ft. Overnight remains “Hundreds of acres”
even though the illustrative map intentionally holds its last supported footprint.

Homes in path counts cached California OSM building reference points inside the
same 1.4× radial envelope used by the map at the selected step's target size.
The count does not follow the brief visual tween. It includes the fire footprint,
is independent of layer visibility, and is labeled buildings, not verified homes.
Unknown initial footprints produce an unavailable value, not a zero-risk claim.
The data is incomplete and is not a verified July 2021 building inventory.

Confidence, volunteer responses, response ETAs, weather, risk ratings, and
Firewatch time gains remain unavailable. The original prototype confidence
algorithm is retained but is not fed unsupported Dixie evidence. The workbook's
5:12 PM 911 call is a historical benchmark, not a responder arrival or measured
Firewatch advantage. Original source and assumption labels remain in the Data tab.

Nearby help lists California stations, bases, water references and separately
identified unverified operators, with sources. Locations do not establish active
availability. Terrain is draped; fuel is only qualitative workbook context;
historical weather and road containment effects are not modeled.

The drone controls and window manager are unchanged. No workbook drone-confirmation
step exists, so the window explicitly reports unavailable footage and retains
SIMULATED FEED labeling without inventing a flight. Manual checks: all steps in
Simple/Full view, KPI tooltips, Nearby help, Likely path/buildings toggles, drone
open/swap/close, legend, and calculator. No browser automation was run.
